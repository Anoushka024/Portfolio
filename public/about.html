Beyond technical skills, I value clarity, consistency, and continuous learning. I enjoy refining my understanding, simplifying complex concepts, and adapting to new challenges. My goal is to grow as a developer with a strong foundation in cybersecurity, building projects that are not only functional but also secure and reliable.
